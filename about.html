About Ende Enat

Our Mission

To improve maternal and newborn health in Ethiopia by providing accessible, reliable, and professional postpartum care services through a digital platform that connects mothers with trained caregivers, healthcare professionals, and support resources during the critical recovery period after childbirth.

Problem Identified

Many mothers in Ethiopia receive little or no professional care after leaving the hospital following childbirth. While healthcare facilities focus heavily on delivery services, postpartum follow-up care is often limited. At the same time, traditional family support systems are becoming less available due to urbanization, migration, and changing lifestyles.

As a result, many mothers face recovery challenges, emotional stress, and health complications without adequate support. Newborns may also miss essential monitoring and guidance during their first weeks of life. This gap in postpartum care creates risks for both mothers and babies and highlights the need for accessible and continuous support services.
